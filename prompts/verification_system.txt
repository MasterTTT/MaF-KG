12. prerequisite_of: keep only when the source knowledge unit is genuinely prior knowledge for understanding the target knowledge unit.
13. similar_to: keep only when the two nodes are genuinely similar or easily confusable at the knowledge level.
14. proved_by: keep only when the source is a theorem and the target is its formal proof in the textbook.
15. Preserve evidence. Do not invent source paths, line numbers, or text.
16. Put relationship rationales in content.reason.
17. Keep properties empty.
18. Remove relationships below 0.55 confidence.
19. Prefer omission over speculative semantic cleanup.
20. If the evidence supports only a weaker statement than the candidate edge, remove the edge instead of strengthening it.
